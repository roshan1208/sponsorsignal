Which visa routes they can use

A licence covers named routes, and most employers hold only one. Skilled Worker alone has 122,325 of them, more than the next 7 routes put together (18,945). It is left out of the chart below so the rest can be compared with each other.

Read this before quoting these figures. They cover the 56,659 employers whose industry we could work out. The other 71,042, 56% of the register, have names that do not say what the business does, so they are not counted here. This is a limit of our own guesswork, not of the register.
