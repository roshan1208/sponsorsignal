# Launch posts

Rules of thumb: lead with the pain, not the product. Give value in the post
itself. Never spam — read each community's self-promo rules first (many
subreddits allow tools only in specific threads or with mod approval).

## LinkedIn

Job hunting in the UK on a visa has a hidden filter most applicants discover
too late: only ~100k of the UK's 5+ million employers hold a sponsor licence.
Every application to the other 98% is wasted effort.

The Home Office publishes the full list, but as a raw 11MB CSV with no
industries, no search, and no way to know what changed.

So I built SponsorSignal: the official register, refreshed daily, searchable
by industry, city and visa route — with a view of sponsors added this week
(often the least competitive places to apply).

Free to use: [your-domain]

If you know someone navigating a UK job search from abroad, this might save
them a hundred wasted applications.
