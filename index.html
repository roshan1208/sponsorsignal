Common questions

What does it mean if a company is on this register?

The organisation holds an active Home Office sponsor licence, so it is legally able to sponsor workers on the listed routes. It doesn't guarantee they are hiring right now, or that any application will succeed — it tells you where sponsorship is possible.

Where does the data come from?

Directly from the official GOV.UK "Register of licensed sponsors: workers" publication. We download the latest file automatically every day, clean it, and add industry tags to make it searchable. Nothing here is edited by hand.

What does the A rating mean?

An A rating means the sponsor is in good standing with the Home Office. A B rating means the sponsor has been downgraded and is working through an action plan — they generally can't issue new certificates until restored to an A rating.

How do the industry tags work?

Industry tags are our own classification, inferred from organisation names, because the official register doesn't include industries. They are a search aid, not official data — always verify details with the employer.

SponsorSignal is an independent search tool built on open public data from GOV.UK. It is not affiliated with the Home Office and is not immigration advice.
