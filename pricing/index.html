What this cannot do

Worth knowing before you pay rather than after.

It is not immigration advice.
We report what two public registers say. Whether you or a candidate qualifies for anything is a question for a regulated adviser.
A Companies House signal is not proof.
Employers are matched to company records by name. Where a name is held by more than one company we show nothing rather than guess, but a match is still a pointer to the official record, not a verdict.
We cannot tell you who is hiring.
A licence means an employer can sponsor a visa. It does not mean they have a vacancy, and no register anywhere records that.
The history starts when we started.
Daily records begin 31 August 2026 and Companies House snapshots begin September 2026. We will not pretend to know what happened before we were looking.
