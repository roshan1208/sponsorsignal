Privacy

What this site collects, which is very little, and who else sees it. In plain English, because a notice nobody reads protects nobody.

The short version

Searches on the public site stay on your device. In the signed-in customer portal, a search is sent to the service to find matching employers but is not kept as search history.

Personal data is collected only when you choose to give it. This can be an email address for alerts or business contact details for a trial or paid service. It is never sold, rented or shared for marketing.

What is collected

If you sign up for the weekly email

Your email address, plus the city and industry you picked, so the email can be about your area instead of the whole country. That is all. No name, no phone number, no address.

Sign-up is double opt-in: you are not subscribed until you click the link in the confirmation email. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops the emails; you can also ask me to delete the subscriber record.

If you ask for a trial or paid watchlist

I keep the business and account details you provide, the employers you choose, delivery preferences, service status and the customer and subscription identifiers returned by Paddle. This is needed to provide the service, keep it secure and maintain an audit trail. Paddle keeps the payment details and issues the payment documents.

If your organisation uses team features, I also keep each member's work email, name, role, portfolio responsibilities and report-delivery history. Adviser report links contain a read-only evidence snapshot and expire automatically; anyone holding an unexpired link can open it, so workspace members should share it only with the intended client. Enterprise API secrets are shown once and only a cryptographic hash is retained. Staged single sign-on settings contain the organisation domain, issuer URL and client identifier, but no identity-provider password or client secret.

Do not send candidate names, immigration records, case notes or any other personal data about the people you work with. A watchlist needs employer names and company numbers only.

If you provide an employer policy statement

For the invitation-only employer policy pilot, I keep the representative's name, work email domain, exact sponsor record, submitted policy fields, verification and review history. The email address is used for a one-time domain check and operational follow-up. It is never included in the public policy receipt.

A verified work email does not prove authority to speak for an employer. Every statement therefore also receives a separate identity review before publication. Published receipts show only the approved policy fields, dates, source link and limitations.

If you only search

Nothing that identifies you. The site records anonymous page views and fixed product actions, such as opening an employer or creating a comparison, so I know which parts are useful. Analytics events never contain search text, employer names, email addresses or filter values. There are no cookies, advertising trackers or visitor profiles.

Your browser stores the theme you chose, your shortlist, recent search settings, application tracker entries and the register version you last saw. Job titles, salary figures, occupation codes, statuses and notes in the application tracker stay on your own device and are not readable by me. A pasted job advert is processed in the page to fill the checker; the advert text is not uploaded or saved. A shortlist leaves the device only if you deliberately create a share link; that link contains the employer identifiers needed to reproduce the list.

If you use the browser extension

The extension runs only when you choose its toolbar button. It temporarily reads the active page to suggest the employer, job title, location and advertised salary. Those suggestions are processed in the extension and shown for correction before anything is transferred.

If you choose to continue, only the reviewed fields, salary period and website name are placed in the KnowYourSponsor page address after the # sign and read by the job checker in your browser. The full advert, page address and browsing history are not transferred or retained by the extension.

Live sponsor evidence is optional. If you enable it, the browser gives the extension permission to request public files from knowyoursponsor.co.uk until you remove that permission in the browser's extension settings. It requests the first-letter search and identity shards needed for the suggestion and, after an exact match, the same fixed file of approved employer-policy receipts. The request does not contain the employer name, job title, salary, vacancy address or browsing history, and each request is sent without website credentials or a referrer. As with any web request, the server can receive normal network data such as an IP address and the shard letter requested.

The extension has no permanent access to job websites, no background process, advertising or analytics. Use of information received through the extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What your browser reveals to servers

Loading any page means your device asks a server for files, and that request carries your IP address. That is true of every website. The companies below see those requests.

Why I am allowed to hold it

Under UK GDPR there has to be a lawful basis for processing personal data. There are three here:

  • Consent, for the weekly email. You asked for it, you confirmed it by clicking a link, and you can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Legitimate interests, for anonymous page counts and server logs. Knowing which pages are used is necessary to run the site, and it cannot identify you.
  • Contract, when you ask for a trial or buy a monitoring service. Your contact details and instructions are needed to provide the report you requested.
  • Legitimate interests, when an employer representative participates in the policy pilot. Contact and verification records are needed to prevent impersonation, review the claim and maintain an accountable evidence trail.

International processing

Some suppliers named above operate outside the UK. Where personal data is processed internationally, the supplier's privacy terms explain the location and the contractual or adequacy safeguards it uses. Contact me if you want the relevant details before providing business information.

How long it is kept

Your email address is kept while you subscribe. Unsubscribing stops future emails, and you can ask for the subscriber record to be deleted. Anonymous page-view counts are aggregate numbers with nothing personal in them, so they are kept indefinitely.

Trial watchlists and related messages are deleted 90 days after the last contact. Paid watchlist records are kept while the service is active and for 90 days afterwards, unless a longer period is needed to resolve a billing or legal issue. Paddle keeps transaction records under its own legal and tax obligations. You can ask for watchlist data to be deleted sooner once the service ends.

Employer policy contact and review records are kept while a statement is under review or published and for 12 months after it expires, is withdrawn or is rejected. A minimal non-public audit record may be retained longer where needed to prevent a disputed statement from being republished.

Your rights

UK GDPR gives you the right to ask what data I hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to get a copy of it, and to object to it being processed. There is no charge and I will answer within one month.

For an email subscriber, this is usually one email address and one city. A business customer may also have contact messages, watchlist instructions and sent reports. Unsubscribing stops newsletter emails; contact me if you also want the subscriber record deleted.

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at hello@knowyoursponsor.co.uk.

If you think I have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk. You are welcome to raise it with me first, but you do not have to.
