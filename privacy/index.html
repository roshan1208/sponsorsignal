Privacy

What this site collects, which is very little, and who else sees it. In plain English, because a notice nobody reads protects nobody.

The short version

Search terms stay on your device and are not sent to me. The whole register is downloaded and searched on your own device.

Personal data is collected only when you choose to give it. This can be an email address for alerts or business contact details for a trial or paid service. It is never sold, rented or shared for marketing.

What is collected

If you sign up for the weekly email

Your email address, plus the city and industry you picked, so the email can be about your area instead of the whole country. That is all. No name, no phone number, no address.

Sign-up is double opt-in: you are not subscribed until you click the link in the confirmation email. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops the emails; you can also ask me to delete the subscriber record.

If you ask for a trial or paid watchlist

I keep the business contact details you provide, the employers you ask me to monitor, our instructions and the reports sent to you. This is needed to provide the service, keep an audit trail and issue invoices.

Do not send candidate names, immigration records, case notes or any other personal data about the people you work with. A watchlist needs employer names and company numbers only.

If you only search

Nothing that identifies you. The site records anonymous page views and fixed product actions, such as opening an employer or creating a comparison, so I know which parts are useful. Analytics events never contain search text, employer names, email addresses or filter values. There are no cookies, advertising trackers or visitor profiles.

Your browser stores the theme you chose, your shortlist, recent search settings and the register version you last saw. These stay on your own device and are not readable by me. A shortlist leaves the device only if you deliberately create a share link; that link contains the employer identifiers needed to reproduce the list.

What your browser reveals to servers

Loading any page means your device asks a server for files, and that request carries your IP address. That is true of every website. The companies below see those requests.

Why I am allowed to hold it

Under UK GDPR there has to be a lawful basis for processing personal data. There are three here:

  • Consent, for the weekly email. You asked for it, you confirmed it by clicking a link, and you can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing.
  • Legitimate interests, for anonymous page counts and server logs. Knowing which pages are used is necessary to run the site, and it cannot identify you.
  • Contract, when you ask for a trial or buy a monitoring service. Your contact details and instructions are needed to provide the report you requested.

International processing

Some suppliers named above operate outside the UK. Where personal data is processed internationally, the supplier's privacy terms explain the location and the contractual or adequacy safeguards it uses. Contact me if you want the relevant details before providing business information.

How long it is kept

Your email address is kept while you subscribe. Unsubscribing stops future emails, and you can ask for the subscriber record to be deleted. Anonymous page-view counts are aggregate numbers with nothing personal in them, so they are kept indefinitely.

Trial watchlists and related messages are deleted 90 days after the last contact. Paid watchlist records are kept while the service is active and for 90 days afterwards, unless tax or accounting law requires an invoice record to be kept longer. You can ask for watchlist data to be deleted sooner once the service ends.

Your rights

UK GDPR gives you the right to ask what data I hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to get a copy of it, and to object to it being processed. There is no charge and I will answer within one month.

For an email subscriber, this is usually one email address and one city. A business customer may also have contact messages, watchlist instructions and sent reports. Unsubscribing stops newsletter emails; contact me if you also want the subscriber record deleted.

To exercise any of these rights, contact me at hello@knowyoursponsor.co.uk.

If you think I have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK regulator, at ico.org.uk. You are welcome to raise it with me first, but you do not have to.
